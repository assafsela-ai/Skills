# Meeting room booking — sample intake

Fictional support exchange. All times are UTC.

## Initial report

> Room booking gets stuck sometimes.

## Follow-up

**1. What did you do just before it got stuck, and what did you see?**

> In the web app, I searched for a room, chose an available slot for the next day, and clicked Reserve once. I expected a confirmation with the room and time. The button kept showing a spinner for over a minute. I saw no error or confirmation.

**2. Which browser and device were you using, and how often did it happen?**

> Chrome on a Windows laptop. I tried three new bookings for three different slots in the same session. In two attempts the spinner stayed. In the other attempt I got a confirmation.

**3. Did the reservation appear afterward? When did you last see the problem?**

> The last time was May 18, 2026 at 10:12 UTC. I refreshed and opened My Reservations. The room and slot from that attempt were listed there. I did not check the other attempt that got stuck.
